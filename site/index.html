Traceable by design

What the archive does — and does not — claim

01

One record per work

Repeated search results and alternate DOI records are joined before a paper appears here.

02

A recorded decision

Identity, source, relevance, topic and the public explanation must all be checked and recorded.

03

No automatic judgement

Search tools can find candidates and prepare data. They cannot silently decide that a paper belongs in the review.

04

The search stays open

Repeated database, reference and citation searches show when new work becomes rare; they never prove that nothing else exists.

Open-access scope. The assessed corpus covers scholarship with lawfully accessible full text. The operational register also displays records awaiting access verification. A free abstract is insufficient. Access is checked separately from scientific relevance and publication approval. Coverage may underrepresent older work and disciplines with fewer open deposits.

Versioned metadata and inclusion provenance are generated from governed registries only. No full texts are redistributed.
