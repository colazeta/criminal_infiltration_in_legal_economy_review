Traceable by design

What the archive does — and does not — claim

01

One canonical work

Each visible item is deduplicated into the canonical work registry. Primary and alternate DOI manifestations are resolved before publication.

02

Complete publication gate

One current eligible decision, a discovery event, verified identifier, controlled topic and approved public annotation are all required.

03

No hidden promotion

Automated retrieval may create leads, but it cannot turn them into included evidence. Ambiguity remains in the editorial layer.

04

No completeness claim

E0 establishes the seed corpus. Saturation can only be assessed after repeated database and citation-chain executions.
