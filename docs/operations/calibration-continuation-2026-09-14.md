## Limits that prevent end-to-end acceptance

The current `focused.py` and its converter are explicitly abstract-only. They
cannot certify exhaustive variables, multiple-study relations or full-text
source consultation. This run is the executable abstract stage of calibration,
NOT the complete heterogeneous/full-text benchmark requested by the owner.
A favourable result must not activate the extractor, import a calibration receipt
or increment Completed. Full-text and hard-case evidence must be assessed
separately with a tested full-text-capable extractor.

The current generated `prompt_sha256` hashes the entire source-bound request,
whereas the new calibration lookup matches an exact prompt hash across papers.
Before any cross-paper acceptance, engineering must separate stable versioned
prompt/decoder configuration identity from the exact per-paper request hash,
retaining both with their proper provenance. Historical proposals are immutable
and must not be rewritten to adopt that future convention.

The current converter also leaves unsupported mandatory fields explicitly
`not_verifiable`; those cannot be waived to satisfy completion. A final reference
assessment and human acceptance must test the complete intended scope, not merely
JSON validity or positive class labels.
